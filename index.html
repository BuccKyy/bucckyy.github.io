About

I'm an AI engineer at MaxflowAI, where I design and ship multi-agent systems for enterprise clients — B2B sales automation, inventory intelligence, HR and event operations.

My path started in biomedical signal processing: a B.Eng in Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering (University of Science, VNU-HCM, GPA 3.4/4.0) and research on cuffless blood pressure estimation from PPG signals — from deep learning models down to STM32 firmware.

That range is the throughline: I like building systems that work end to end, and proving they work with deterministic evaluation rather than vibes. Calculations belong in Python; LLMs write the words.
